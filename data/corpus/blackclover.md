Morgen dodges the attack and faces Yami while Mereoleona fights Moris and quickly loses an arm to his Modification Magic. Mereoleona enters Hellfire Incarnate and begins destroying Moris's arms, but Moris reveals that his new body can dismantle anything it touches instantly and regenerates faster than Mereoleona can destroy it. With Fuegoleon unable to come to his sister's aid while fighting several Guardian Angels by himself, the other Crimson Lions resort to sacrificing themselves as human shields to protect Mereoleona from Moris's lethal touch. Fuegoleon realizes that Moris's regeneration and dismantling speed have limits, and that his sister has realized the same thing so she's challenging them with a life risking gambit. With the sacrifice of the Crimson Lions prolonging the fight, Hellfire Incarnate is able to build more power, and Mereoleona vows to defeat Moris in tribute for her fallen comrades. Acier defeats Nozel and Silver Eagles, but Noelle, arrives to their rescue before she can kill them. Noelle is revealed to have obtain made a contract with a legendary dragon, Leviathan, with help from Kiato and Kahono, who have also joined the fight, and faces her mother with a new Valkyrie Dress. Yuno continues to fight Lucius and is able to keep up with his speed and predictions. When Yuno blocks Lucius' attempt to neutralize him with Soul Magic and counterattack, the latter realizes the former has grown stronger than any of his predictions foresaw. He also discovers that other magic knights have defied his predictions and are continuing to fight long after the point they were supposed to be wiped out. Lucius wonders what could have caused such a change and realizes that he cannot read the future because it one that has been influenced by Asta. While Lucius is distracted by this revelation, Yuno lands a decisive slash through him and defeats him. However, a second Lucius attacks and reveals that with the combined power of his family's magics, he is now able to clone himself. Multiple Lucius clones appear and used their combined Gravity Magic to raise the entire capital in the sky while the real Lucius watches from a floating palace. As the Lucius clones scatter across the battlfield, Noelle refuses to lose hope, knowing that the rest of the Black Bulls are working to bring back Asta. Before Judgment Day, the Black Bulls visited the Witches' Forest to seek help from the Witch Queen, who revealed Asta was alive and trapped in a foreign land. Dorothy Unsworth proposed combining her, Vanessa, and the Queen's power to create the spell Door of Fate, to reach Asta. While the arrival of the Lucius clones turns the battle in their favor, Marx Francois and Mimosa make a public announcement, revealing that the magic knights have prevented any civilian causalities and that there still is hope. They urge the citizens to protect themselves, while the magic knights, including Sekke Bronzazza keep fighting. In the Land of the Sun, Asta prepares to be brought back, but when Ryuya checks on the status of the Black Bulls with his Tengentsu, he sees Damnatio, now a Paladin, and several Guardian Angels invading the Witches' Forest. Lucius is revealed to have Damnatio to stop the Black Bulls from retrieving Asta in case he somehow survived. Damnatio easily overwhelms the witches' defenses, but several of the Black Bulls stand in his way before he can interrupt the ritual. The Black Bulls work together to fight Damnatio using mirror copies of Grey to keep them healed until Damnatio figures out their strategy and mortally wounds Grey and Gauche Adlai. Even without Grey, Secre is able to seal her squad's injuries and allow them to keep fighting. The squad launches renewed attacks that destroy the Guardian Angels, but they are no match for Damnatio when he unveils his second magic attribute. They are defeated one after another and Damnatio finally breaks into the ritual chamber. However, their efforts succeed in stalling him long enough for Finral Roulacase, to reach Asta and bring him back through the Door of Fate, along with Ichika at Ryuya's suggestion. With his improved control of his Anti Magic, Asta is able to counter all of Damnatio's spells and defeat him with Zetten. Damnatio is turned back to human while the Witch Queen fully heals the injured Black Bulls at the cost of most of her power. The Queen asks Vanessa to succeed her as the next ruler of the forest, but Dorothy offers to take the position instead. Asta then reveals that during his training, Ryuya theorized he could impart Anti Magic into people like he can with objects using the Demon-Dweller Sword and uses this new ability to grant Anti Magic to his squad. Back in the capital, Morgen reveals his second attribute, Dark Magic, and defeats Yami while Yuno is overwhelmed by fighting two Lucius clones at once. Fortunately, the Black Bulls and Ichika arrive and spread across the battlefields to fight Lucius' forces. Asta and Yuno reunite and team up to fight the Lucius clones. More Lucius clones come to fight Asta and Yuno and boast that even with Asta back, they still have the advantage with several dozen Guardian Angel, three Paladins, and eleven clones. Before the Black Bulls went to the capital, Asta formally introduce them to Ichika, who reveals that the Anti-Magic they were given is limited and will likely only last for two big attacks. Using their new Anti Magic and teamwork, Luck Voltia and Magna Swing defeat several Guardian Angels and a Lucius clone.
