# jigokuraku — Post-Anime Corpus

## [series=jigokuraku | page=Hōrai_Arc | section=Arc Chronology] Arc Chronology

The Hōrai Arc (ほうらい編, Hōrai-hen) is the third arc of Jigokuraku. The arc focuses on the alliance of the surviving criminals and executioners invading Hōrai to retrieve the Elixir of Life and escape the island. However, Lord Tensen anticipates their arrival and prepares to offer them as sacrifices to their master while Rien, the leader of Lord Tensen, prepares to depart to Japan with the perfected Elixir of Life and create more of the elixir from Japan's populace. Chaos soon erupts with the arrival of the second landing party and one of the members of Lord Tensen, Zhu Jin, resorting to assimilating themselves with the Banko.

## [series=jigokuraku | page=Hōrai_Arc | section=Infiltrating Hōrai] Infiltrating Hōrai

Fuchi presents the information Mei relayed to him regarding the palace's layout and the under-sea monsters surrounding the island. According to Mei, the place has a floodgate containing large number of ships that they can use to avoid the under-sea monsters and the Tan is located within Rien's laboratory but is warned that the palace's layout is constantly changing. Knowing that they will soon enter enemy territory, Shion pulls Gabimaru and Yuzuriha over to have them instruct the group in the ways of espionage and how to use Tao. Yuzuriha starts off by giving her lesson on how to hide their presence by using Tao, while Gabimaru instructs them in how to use Tao in battle. Once the lessons were over, the group separates into two teams: the Elixir Retrieval Team and the Escape Route Securing Team. After the teams were formed, Gantetsusai recommends that Mei not wait for the Elixir Team and have her escape alone. Gabimaru feels that Mei wanted to do something before leaving the island and asks her if that was true. Mei answers that she wishes to see Rien and Jofuku to ask them why they underwent a major change in behavior in the past and then leave the island to live a normal life. Having said that, Gabimaru promises her that he will make it happen. The Elixir Team infiltrates the palace. In Hōrai, Chōbei joins Lord Tensen in a ritual and gets into conflict with Ju Fa. Rien breaks up their squabble and has Chōbei follow him to his laboratory to show him the Banko, a large Waitanhua flower meant to harvest more Tan, and explains that with Chōbei's Tao he will be able to help the Banko create the perfect Tan he seeks. Chōbei begins to terrifyingly ask himself why Lord Tensen needed to create more Tan and worries that Tōma might get involved with their plans. Rien then brings out his dissection tools and asks Chōbei which part of his body he should extract first. Meanwhile, Gabimaru, Yuzuriha, Sagiri, and Mei manage to sneak their way into the palace and signals the others that it was okay to enter. While during their planning stages, the group learns from Mei that the palace houses six palaces based on the Five Training Methods of Immortality for each member of Lord Tensen, with the Tan elixir located at Rien's laboratory known as the Rentan Palace. She explains that Rien will be in the room where Jofuku is located since the Waitanhua flower drops at night, making it easy for them to avoid a sudden encounter. As planned, the Elixir Team uses their Tao to get a feel of the layout inside the castle and eliminates the Dōshi guards by suppressing their presence. Once they manage to enter the room, Gabimaru senses the Tao of the layout and sees that there is no one inside, however, he starts to see Tao being gathered in one spot and is revealed to be Ran who was concealing his Tao. Ran reveals that Lord Tensen have been expecting the survivors arrival to the palace after Chōbei's earlier warning and have ambushed them in their respective palaces to complete the Rite of Just Consumption, a ritual where they capture the Tao of the humans to sacrifice them to Jofuku. Ran then tries to convince Mei to join back with Lord Tensen after revealing that Rien has finally completed the Elixir of Life, much to Gabimaru's shock. Mei tells Ran that she is different from the other Tensen and that she has come with the purpose of saying goodbye to them before leaving the island. With their plan of not encountering Lord Tensen failed, the survivors move towards their second strategy of engaging in battle with them.

## [series=jigokuraku | page=Departure_Arc | section=Arc Chronology] Arc Chronology

Ultimately, Gabimaru and Yuzuriha decide that Gantetsusai will be the on to receive the pardon due to his famous reputation. With this decided, Jikka suggests in having the ones that will be departing to abandon their names and former lives, which Yuzuriha connected to the prophecy of the I Ching Gui Fa warned her about, and that he will handle the rest from here on. After Shion, Nurugai, and Yuzuriha depart on their row boats, Sagiri and Gabimaru happily go their separate ways without saying a word to each other. Later, the shogun becomes informed that a ship has returned from the island with only two men and woman one onboard. Gantetsusai presenting himself as the only surviving criminal. After ten days had passed, Jikka reports that they managed to retrieve the Elixir of Life and presents Gantetsusai as the only surviving criminal, falsely stating that he executed both Gabimaru, Aza Chōbei, Yuzuriha, and Nurugai as per his duties. With matters settled, the shogun rewards Gantetsusai with the pardon and makes his leave. Before allowing Aoki to leave, Jikka asks him if the arrangements concerning the Iwagakure chief were settled. Aoki answers yes, reporting that the leader was beheaded, upon being told by Jikka that the shinobi dispatched on the mission were ordered to carry out the leader's own goals, and that his body was transformed into a tree after forcing him to test the effects of the elixir. Aoki becomes disappointed with the results but is convinced by Jikka that the shogun will benefit greatly if he were to become an everlasting tree who could watch over his family throughout the centuries. After finishing upholding his promise, Jikka sees Sagiri thinking about the others and cheers her up by saying that their story had to eventually come to an end and all they can do is hope they live happy lives. Sagiri then happily sheds tears upon hearing these words. Gabimaru and Yui walking off together. Meanwhile at sea, Tōma is revealed to have gotten off the island after procuring a ship of his own and is also shown being accompanied by an unknown person. In the forest, Nurugai happily travels with Shion by her side. After the arrangements were set by Jikka, Gabimaru walks to the designated meeting spot and becomes shocked to finally see Yui again. Yui also sees Gabimaru and becomes overjoyed as her husband runs towards her direction. With the married couple happily reunited, Gabimaru and Yui walk off to start their new life together.
