# chainsaw-man — Post-Anime Corpus

## [series=chainsaw-man | page=International_Assassins_arc | section=Anime] Anime

The International Assassins Arc (国際暗殺者編, Kokusai Ansatsusha Hen?), also officially known as the Assassins Arc (刺客篇, Shikaku Hen?), is the sixth arc of the Public Safety Saga of Chainsaw Man.

## [series=chainsaw-man | page=International_Assassins_arc | section=Synopsis] Synopsis

While asleep, Denji's having another dream of the door Pochita warned him to never open. Makima visits Aki, Power and Denji at their apartment and invites them on a trip to Enoshima, as multiple Devil Hunters come through the door. After Denji's fight with Reze, his various battles are broadcast on TV, and he is told by Makima that a number of assassins will be coming to Japan to kill him, while saying that their trip to Enoshima is postponed. Makima prepares to put a number of Devil Hunters as Denji's bodyguards while telling Aki that they're going to need everyone's strength to fight the Gun Devil next year, so he should avoid dying during the onslaught. At a restaurant, Angel has a conversation with Aki, in which he reveals more information about the Devils to him, including a sound every Devil from Special Division 4 heard before, the sound of a roaring engine from Chainsaw Man. Elsewhere, three brothers get recruited for a job by the federal agents in America to kill and bring Denji to them in exchange for 2 million dollars. While Aldo is hesitant, the other brothers gladly accept the job, declaring themselves immortal. Meanwhile, at a forest in the Soviet Union, Tolka and Master are seen hunting wolves. Master tests his expertise in taking lives and tells him about a job of killing a 16-year-old boy while showing him a picture of Denji, to which Tolka complies, saying he can do it. In China, Quanxi is shown having sex with four fiends at a hotel when they get recruited by the General to capture Denji, saying it will be their most dangerous job yet. Quanxi agrees while asking what her reward will be. Pingtsi excitedly asks for 100 donuts. Long demands to drink blood. In a frenzy, Cosmo replies with "Halloween", while Tsugihagi is dead silent, which prompts Quanxi to say she wants to secure human rights and education for her fellow fiend girlfriends. While at Public Safety, Makima discusses the matter with Kishibe, asking him how they should deal with Quanxi, to which he replies that he's not sure himself, as, if humanity held a bare-knuckle fighting tournament, Quanxi would take first place. Makima dismisses him, saying that Santa Claus from Germany will be a bigger problem to deal with. Santa Claus is shown in Munich, Germany, being told that "Christmas has come a little early", while he remarks on who's getting the present. That being Denji, with his newfound popularity in Japan, Santa Claus says he will accept the job if he gets to adopt three children to use for contracts and one for pleasure. Denji is shown lying down, asking himself why everyone's after his heart and questioning who Pochita is to begin with. He then says he can't go on his trip with Makima because of the mess he found himself in, after which Aki comes to the room and sits on top of his back, which annoys Denji. Aki says that their trip was only postponed and that Makima never said it was canceled, while Aki and Denji agree to take down all the assassins targeting him. Near a lake, Denji and Power are accompanied by Aki, Angel, Kusakabe, Tamaoki and Hirofumi Yoshida, who are tasked to protect Denji from any potential threats, while under constant surveillance. In a flashback, Kusakabe, Tamaoki and Yoshida introduce themselves to Denji and Makima says there will be three more agents from Kyoto who will be sticking close to him for the time being. While introducing himself, Denji disrespects Kusakabe, which makes him visibly annoyed with his behavior. In the meantime, Michiko Tendo, Yutaro Kurose and another devil hunter named Subaru are having a conversation about keeping Denji locked at home. Subaru explains that's not possible as Devils have keener senses of smell than humans, and they can't hide him from people with Devil contracts either. While on their way to Tokyo, Tendo and Kurose told Subaru that they wanted to talk about Makima, but they suddenly get ambushed by the American Thugs, who crashed their car with road spikes. They are subsequently shot and killed, while Joey and the eldest brother joke about them. Aldo is visibly disgusted and vomits while saying it was a piece of cake. The two brothers continue joking and laughing about the situation. The eldest brother uses his Skin Devil contract to impersonate Kurose, while Aldo tells him to fix his accent. "Kurose" then asks them "Let's go, shall we?". The group goes to a Burger Shop to dine, where Master and Tolka also visit. Master stabs Denji three times with a nail while avoiding detection by the Devil Hunters. Master says she'll leave the fourth stab to him and that if he succeeds with his mission, she'll introduce him to the Devils she has contracts with, after which she enjoys eating a burger. In the meantime, Kobeni and Galgali have just killed the Grape Devil and, after being done, they take a break. Galgali treats Kobeni to an ice cream and says he's really nice despite being a Violence Fiend. He says he enjoys peace much like Kobeni, but they are suddenly interrupted by "Kurose", who comes with Subaru's car and tells them both of his partners were killed and needs help. They run off to Public Safety HQ, while "Kurose" calls them stupid for believing him without seeing his badge. Later, Aki tells Power and Denji they are being used as bait to draw any assassins targeting Denji, much to their protests. The three Devil Hunters meet up with Denji's personal security while "Kurose" continues with the act, trying to get close to Denji, but is killed by Power, who stole Kobeni's car. Their car crash shocks everyone around, besides Tamaoki, who recognizes the assassin and says that his contract with the Skin Devil allowed him to steal the appearance of a dead person. Power gets excited about seeing through the enemy's disguise, while Yoshida says they're on the lookout for the other two brothers, who might have already replaced any of them. Kusakabe says they need to verify their identities by showing their Devil contracts somewhere quiet. While Kobeni's still in the car in shock, the disguised Aldo and Joey watch in horror. In an allyway, Yoshida executes Joey, while Aldo avoids detection by Yoshida, mistaking him for a civilian. Quanxi and her harem of fiends arrive in Japan, after crashing their car into another car. Cosmo uses her power on the driver, after which he starts repeating "Halloween" over and over again. Quanxi and her harem, consisting of Long, Cosmo, Pingtsi and Tsugihagi, prepare to hunt Denji. Pingtsi then demands the group visit a sushi restaurant, to which Quanxi agrees. During the group's banter, Quanxi gets exhausted and says she is out of energy for the day, leaving the job for tomorrow. Elsewhere, Aldo disguises himself as Kurose to avoid the police and visits an old friend of Kurose. Tomono and "Kurose" talk about their worries all night while eating and drinking. Tomono says he'll quit his job and "Kurose" should do the same, so he doesn't wind up dead like his brother. "Kurose" thinks to himself about his "Immortal Brothers" and says that a pro should always get the job done. On the street, Santa Claus begins the battle with the Devil Hunters by transforming the nearby civilians into dolls. Aldo, Tolka and Master prepare to hunt Denji, while Quanxi's having sex with her girlfriends at a hotel. Makima says she will finish cleaning up the dolls at a building and tells Beam and Princi to assist Denji and the devil hunters while retrieving as many bodies as possible. Santa Claus looks over from a café and commands the doll army to take out the group while they go inside a mall. The dolls transform their arms into blades and go inside the building, only to be ambushed and destroyed by Kusakabe, who used his Stone Devil contract. After a sudden, a massive doll army bursts through the door, catching the Devil Hunters by surprise. Three dolls catch up to Power, but Denji and Power destroy them with kicks and her Blood Scythe. They question Kusakabe why aren't they turning into dolls, to which he replies that only humans should be able to be transformed. Nakamura defends them by using the Fox Devil's paw to destroy several dolls on their way. Tamaoki devises a plan to take out Santa Claus. Master is sitting at a theme park nearby, saying that it will all be over soon, while Tolka prepares the nail to stab Denji with, while Aldo's in the bathroom panicking. An announcement tells the people inside that the department store isn't safe anymore, as Santa Claus sends his doll army to attack the group. Kobeni's distressed about her damaged car outside. At a hotel, Pingtsi says that Santa Claus has arrived in Japan and that they should put it off for tomorrow as they're tired from having sex all night, to which Quanxi replies that she'll mess them up even more. Angel defends the group by using a sword made from people's stolen lifespans. Quanxi begins her massacre by killing every devil hunter and doll in sight. After reaching the floor the Devil Hunters are in, Nakamura is beheaded, while Aki and Angel defend themselves. Outside, Cosmo and the fiends destroy several of Santa Claus' dolls. Pingtsi says they need to catch up to Quanxi but are intercepted by Kishibe. Pingtsi uses her contract perception ability to determine his contracts, while mocking him and telling her girfriends to kill him. Quanxi fights the Devil Hunters inside the mall and are quickly defeated. Yoshida uses his Octopus Devil contract, summoning an ink cloud to fight Quanxi, but is defeated as well because of her enhanced reflexes. Kishibe comes to the scene while showing that he's captured Long and Pingtsi. Kishibe and Quanxi sit down and have a conversation while Denji and Power hold Long and Pingtsi at knifepoint. While killing the dolls, Galgali asks Kobeni if she's doing okay as she watches her car get destroyed. While Power and Quanxi argue, Beam hurls Long away from Denji, allowing him to escape. Yoshida intercepts, asking Quanxi for a rematch. While fighting, he lands a blow on Quanxi and asks her if her strength is human. While running away with Beam, Denji gets stabbed in the foot by Tolka, as the Curse Devil kills Denji and Tolka kicks Beam in the head. Master congratulates Tolka for completing his mission, while turning him into a doll. Elsewhere, Quanxi throws Yoshida out of the building, but he manages to evade the fall by using his Octopus Devil contract. Santa Claus uses her Hell Devil contract to send everyone inside the department store to Hell. In Hell, Quanxi makes a temporary truce with the Devil Hunters, as the fiends are freaking out. The group is greeted by the arrival of a Primal Fear, the Darkness Devil. The Darkness Devil severs everyone's hands, while the Doll Devil brings Denji to the Darkness Devil. In a flashback, Santa Claus' doll got a note saying to kill Makima. In the present, Santa Claus gets a piece of the Darkness Devil and swallows it whole. The Darkness Devil decapitates Tolka and destroys the Stone Devil along with Kusakabe. Beam feeds Denji his blood and tells him to save them, but is killed by the Darkness Devil. Tamaoki and Quanxi attack the Darkness Devil but Tamaoki gets killed, along with Quanxi, Pingtsi and Long, while Power gets injured by its strikes. Kobeni removed Galgali's mask, so he can fight at full strength, but he's blasted by the Darkness Devil, destroying most of his body. Denji jumps at the Darkness Devil but is defeated by its attacks. While Aki watches his friends get injured, he thinks to himself about losing Himeno, saying he doesn't want to lose any more people. Galgali catches his arm but is stabbed by Darkness' sword, killing him in the process. The Darkness Devil defeats Angel and attacks Aki, before getting jumped by Princi, who gets subdued as well. ## [series=chainsaw-man | page=Gun_Devil_arc | section=Manga] Manga

The Gun Devil Arc (銃の悪魔篇, Jū no Akuma Hen?) is the seventh and penultimate arc of the Public Safety Saga of Chainsaw Man.

## [series=chainsaw-man | page=Gun_Devil_arc | section=Synopsis] Synopsis

Denji, Power and Aki are at their apartment following the battle beforehand. Power is distressed about her encounter with the Darkness Devil in Hell. In a flashback, Makima asked Denji if he wanted to go on their trip, just the two of them, to which he declined as he had to take care of Power. Denji and Power spend their time bonding together. In the morning, Aki informs both of them that he's going to Hokkaido tomorrow to his family's grave. Denji and Power insist on going with him, while a hesitant Aki allows them to come with him. While on their trip, Kishibe takes care of Meowy. While on their trip, the pair annoy Aki on multiple occasions. At a hotel, the trio spend their peaceful moments together. At night, Aki gazes at the snow, after which Denji wakes up and walks towards him. Aki tells him that he didn't have time to think about the bad things while visiting his family's grave like before, because of how much of a nuisance both of them were. Later, Aki has a conversation with Kishibe where he tells him that he wants to withdraw from the expedition to take out the Gun Devil because he doesn't want Denji and Power getting hurt. After the banter at home, Power calmed down. The trio get a call from Makima, who invites them to her office. She tells them that the Gun Devil has already been defeated and confined. While multiple countries around the world possess pieces of its body, fighting him will be like waging a war. Denji is disappointed that he couldn't fight him because it means his wish with Makima won't be fulfilled while Aki's distressed about the situation and talks with the Future Devil. The Future Devil tells him that him and Power will be killed by Denji in the future and after the fact a terrifying devil shall appear. Aki has a conversation with Angel in the hospital about the inevitable future, telling him that he should quit Public Safety. Aki tells him that he doesn't want to quit because Denji, Power and Makima will be away from him. While taking a walk on the beach, Aki and Angel spot Makima near the shore. He then requests he makes a contract with any Devil, so he can protect his friends. Makima then offers him a contract with herself, commanding him to make a contract with her. Angel recalls a forgotten memory of his past in a rural village, where Makima used his powers while taking control of him to slaughter everyone, while Aki accepts. Angel attacks Makima after remembering his tragic past, but is quickly subdued. Makima then takes control of Angel. In the White House, USA, the President sacrifices one year of the lifespans of the American people in exchange for summoning the Gun Devil to kill Makima, while revealing her identity as the Control Devil. The Gun Devil hits the city of Nihako, slaughtering everyone in a Christian church, as it appears in the sky above. The Gun Devil advances towards Makima, slaughtering countless civilians in its path and destroying a large portion of the cities it passes by. The Gun Devil stops and readies his guns to fire at Makima. She gets shot in the head but is unfazed, making a gut halo from her brain and staring at the Gun Devil from a distance. Makima uses a combination of abilities and contracts from the deceased Devil Hunters, Yutaro Kurose, Michiko Tendo, Angel Devil, Aki Hayakawa, Akane Sawatari and Princi to launch an attack on the Gun Devil. She sends the Punishment Devil to its location to torture and kill the Gun Devil. Makima reveals that she "failed" to defeat the Gun Devil to Denji on the phone. She sends the Gun Fiend to Denji and Power's apartment to attack them. Denji and Power couldn't believe her words, deeming the situation absurd, as it's Aki's scent at the entrance of their door. The Gun Fiend attacks Denji, who's hesitant on fighting him. The Gun Fiend kills the nearby civilians while hallucinating about having a snowball fight with Denji and Power. He causes a massive destruction in the city. The civilians feed Denji their blood and plead him to save them. Denji kills Aki, while he's hallucinating about his younger brother Taiyo Hayakawa. The Future Devil appears after the fight, saying that Aki died in the worst possible way for Denji.

The Control Devil Arc (支配の悪魔編, Shihai no Akuma Hen?) is the eighth and final arc of the Public Safety Saga of Chainsaw Man.

Following the death of Aki, Denji is feeling depressed and goes out, while leaving Power alone with Meowy in their new apartment they rented with Aki's money from his will. While outside, he's met up by Makima, who invites him to her apartment. When inside, he is also met by a bunch of dogs. Makima gives Denji tea and a dessert, after which Denji responds by saying that this is the best place in the world. Makima asks Denji what he wants in exchange for defeating the Gun Devil, then Denji answers that he wants to be her dog. Makima says her dogs should follow her with complete obedience and tells him that she invited Power over as it's his birthday. She orders Denji to open the door with the intention of killing her, as he remembers Pochita telling him to never open the door in his dream. Makima then shoots Power while saying "Bang", killing her. Denji can't believe what he witnessed as Makima laughs at him and tells him to take off his shirt. She explains that she wanted to break his contract with Pochita, so she reveals to him that he murdered his father and takes control of Denji. She introduces Denji to his followers in the hallway and says they have all been waiting for his resurrection, as Kishibe commands the Anti-Makima Squad to fire. The forces sacrifice themselves to summon the Hell Devil and cast Makima into Hell, as Makima calls for Chainsaw Man to save her. Pochita takes control of Denji's body, turning into his Full Devil Form and slices the Hell Devil to pieces. The Hell Devil regenerates after drinking a devil hunter's blood and casts Pochita into Hell. Kishibe prepares to shoot at Makima with his gun, but she says it's meaningless as she has a contract with the Prime Minister of Japan and all her injuries get transferred to people with Japanese citizenship. Makima says she's a fan of his and reveals his identity as Chainsaw Man, "The Hero of Hell". She explains his ability to erase devils from existence and wants to use his power to enact a perfect world, where adverse such as Death, War and Hunger don't exist, as she plans to erase them with Chainsaw Man's power. Pochita then takes Kobeni on a date and makes her dance, where she gets a perfect score. Makima reminds Pochita of his battle with the Four Horsemen and the Weapon Devils, where he vanished without a trace and was living in a near-death state and unable to be found even with all her searching. Reze transforms into her Bomb Hybrid form, Katana Man transforms into his Katana Hybrid form, Miri Sugo transforms into his Longsword Hybrid form, Barem Bridge transforms into his Flamethrower Hybrid form, Quanxi transforms into her Bow Hybrid form and the Whip and Spear Hybrids transform as well. Katana Man uses his sword-dash move, but he gets killed by Pochita mere moments later, prompting Makima to say that the battle is unwinnable. Barem attacks with his flamethrowers, scorching the building, but Pochita jumps away. He then gets attacked by Whip, but she gets blasted away by Reze's explosions. While in the sky, Pochita slices Reze to pieces, while Spear attacks him by throwing a spear high in the sky, piercing Pochita. Makima uses her finger bang attack, sending Pochita to space where he removes his heart and throws it back to Earth, while regenerating his entire body. He uses his chains to grapple Makima and the four hybrids and cuts them into pieces with his chainsaws. Pochita returns to Kobeni's location and Makima soon follows, taking control of many of the Devil Hunters present with her chains. In a TV broadcast, Denji's multiple triumphs are broadcast and as the public started viewing Chainsaw Man as a hero, he gets weakened. Makima uses Angel's 1,000-year usage to launch a spear directly at Kobeni, as Pochita takes the blow himself, protecting her, while Makima gets disappointed at how fast she defeated him. Pochita wakes up Power, who's inside his body as Denji drank some of her blood beforehand. She eats a piece of Pochita and manifests from inside his body, emerging in her Blood Devil form. She pierces Makima and the Devil Hunters present with her blood swords and spears. Makima then uses the Zombie Devil, turning the devil hunters into zombies and attacking Power, while Makima blasts her with her finger bangs. Power defies Makima as she thinks of her friendship with Denji and runs away with him, while slaughtering multiple devil hunters in her way. In a dumpster, Denji returns to normal and has a heartfelt conversation with Power where he tells her that he can't live without her. Kobeni then tells Denji that there isn't a life free of bad things except in your dreams, which makes Denji stand up on his feet again. The Justice Devil Arc (正義の悪魔編, Seigi no Akuma Hen?) is the first arc of the Academy Saga of Chainsaw Man, and the ninth arc cumulatively.

## [series=chainsaw-man | page=Justice_Devil_arc | section=Synopsis] Synopsis

Mr. Tanaka introduces the Chicken Devil, Bucky, to his class as a pet. He tells them that in three months the class will have to kill Bucky and eat him because he wants to teach them just how precious life is. Then, a student named Asa Mitaka comes to the classroom only to see the devil sitting on her desk, which makes Asa angry. Asa decides to enjoy eating alone, which prompts their class president to come talk to her. The class is infatuated with Bucky, while Asa wishes he would just die already. After three months had passed, Mr. Tanaka asks a student with devil hunter aspirations to kill Bucky. The class president stands up, saying that they talked with the whole class, except Asa, and decided not to eat him as he has become a friend to them. Mr. Tanaka says that's just what he wanted to hear and that Bucky will become a member of the class. When outside, Asa decides to join them as she wished to make friends, but she falls and crushes Bucky. The scene shocks the students as Bucky's guts spill on the ground. At home, Asa says she wants to die. While in class, the whole classroom is gazing at Asa for what she did. Mr. Tanaka and the class president decide to recruit Asa at night to go visit Bucky's grave. While walking on the street, the class president gets annoyed with her behavior, saying that she's a goody two-shoes. The class president then takes on a monstrous form, revealing that she has made a contract with the Justice Devil. She reveals to Asa that the Justice Devil told her to trip Asa to kill Bucky and laughs at her. The transformed class president attacks Asa, while showing just how much she's angry at her for, "trying to steal Mr. Tanaka" from her. She slices Asa's face with her claws, cutting it in half. A beige potoo-like bird appears on a traffic light and revives Asa. The two are confused as an injured Asa Mitaka stands up from the ground, revealing a facial scar going through her face where the class president sliced her face. ## [series=chainsaw-man | page=War_Devil_arc | section=Manga] Manga

The War Devil Arc (戦争の悪魔編, Sensō no Akuma Hen?) is the sixth and final arc of the Academy Saga of Chainsaw Man, and the fourteenth arc cumulatively.

## [series=chainsaw-man | page=War_Devil_arc | section=Synopsis] Synopsis

In the aftermath of the Aging Devil's defeat, Yoshida reveals that Public Safety has Katana Man, Haruka, Nobana, and Seigi as prisoners. Denji faintly recalls when Pochita took over his body, "Asa" was able to defeat the Chainsaw Devil using a weaponized Statue of Liberty. Asa feels heavy guilt over the many lives taken just as Yoru reveals her role as the War Devil and Asa's "other personality" to Denji. While Yoru gloats over her actions, Yoshida demands her to turn Denji into a weapon for the sake of humanity's survival against the Death Devil. Denji then realizes Public Safety's intentions for him and why his normal life was ruined. Yoru then prepares to attack Yoshida using her Right Gun Gauntlet to showcase her pride in being a Devil who can get away with any horrific action she commits. However, this turns out to be a ruse as she instead destroys the window right next to the group. Yoru decides to show Denji how to have fun as a Devil and drags him outside. Yoru initially wants to kill people but Denji distracts her with thoughts of food. At night, the two decide to eat potstickers Yoru cooked on top of a building. While the world continues to fall into further despair, Yoru informs Denji of what to expect once the Death Devil descends. Denji wonders if he'll be able to meet the Control Devil when that happens, to which Yoru confirms there is a chance she may show up but not as the person Denji once knew. Denji then recalls the promise he made to Power of finding her again despite the fact the two will be complete strangers even if they reunite once more. After being told that he's having fun, Yoru rushes over to sit between Denji's legs and taunts him for the recent misfortunes that occured in his life. Yoru adds that she wants him to be more pitiful once she turns him into a weapon. Yoru also points out that both her and Asa like him, which causes Denji to tear up out of joy that someone still likes him. Yoru then forcibly kisses Denji again not before proposing that if he wants to have sex with her, then he must defeat the Death Devil. Should Denji fail to defeat the Death Devil, Yoru will turn him into a weapon that she will use against her eldest sister instead. The following day, Yoshida and Fumiko attempt to recruit Fami for assistance against the Death Devil but she bluntly declines. Meanwhile, Denji and Yoru are enjoying themselves as the latter destroys various ruined buildings within Tokyo for fun. Just as Yoru attempts to fire at another building, Asa regains control of her body. She demands for Denji to kill her, believing that it is the only way to stop Yoru from committing more horrific acts. However, she is not mentally prepared to die again, to which Denji tells her to focus on the present for the time being. Elsewhere, a new female transfer student with ringed eyes introduces herself to her classmates at Fourth East High School. Before she can use her powers, the imposter Chainsaw Man reappears and confronts her. The student is swiftly defeated in combat but revives herself by absorbing the lifespan of her classmate. She attempts to go another round with the imposter but is once again hacked down to pieces. The imposter assumes that the student is the Death Devil after witnessing her abilities. The student clarifies that she is actually Fami, the Famine Devil. With her cover blown, the real Death Devil, having acted under Fami's alias to conceal her true identity, subdues the two with help from the Falling Devil. The Death Devil proceeds to kill her targets and consume them whole. Afterwards, she resurrects them as brainwashed pawns and sends them to wreak havoc and destruction upon humanity. During a sunset, Asa tells Denji that she now wants to focus on having fun instead of feeling negative all the time. Once Yoru resumes control, she and Denji both decide to ride through the streets of Tokyo using a bike they stole for a post-meal exercise. However, the two are confronted by the Fake Chainsaw Man, who questions Denji if he is aware of the mistake he has made. Elsewhere, Fami attempts to invoke fear from civilians by pelting them with rocks but only succeeds in angering them. The Falling Devil decides to take things up a notch by manipulating gravity, sending multiple civilians to fall horizontally. Before Denji and Fake Chainsaw Man can battle one another, they are bombarded by falling civilians in need of help. Denji attempts to save a nearby female civilian but Yoru instead blasts the woman's hand off. Enraged, Denji realizes that Yoru truly is an irredeemable being as he prepares to fight her. Instead of fighting, Yoru fires at the Fake Chainsaw Man, giving Denji the perfect opening to slice the doppelganger's arm off while midair. Unphased by this, the Fake Chainsaw Man summons a horde of Pseudo-Chainsaw People and assimilates them into his body to replace his missing limbs. By doing so, he forcibily morphs them into large grostesque arms against their own will. This process causes them to regain their free will, prompting them to cry out for help and beg Denji to save them from the enigmatic doppelgänger. Denji is unable to bring himself to the harm the civilians and is sent crashing into a building. Yoru tells Denji that if he wants to win this battle, he must drop any forms of empathy towards them, even if he will soon end up forgotten. The Falling Devil lifts the building the two are inside of and prepares to launch it straight towards several civilians and Fami. Yoru thwarts the Falling Devil's attack by turning the building into a weapon while descending to the ground with Denji. Upon landing, Yoru heads off to confront the Falling Devil while leaving Denji to handle his doppelgänger. Despite failing to negotiate with him, Denji swiftly strikes the Fake Chainsaw Man down, freeing most of the captive civilians in the process. This also results in the Fire Devil revealing itself upon emerging from the imposter's body, having been there the whole time. The Fire Devil explains that the imposter is the older brother of the student Denji chose to not save when he fought the Cockroach Devil. The Fire Devil notes of Denji's apathy towards the two brothers and tells him he must now choose between two options, warning him to not make the same mistake twice before vanishing. Denji then notices Yoru trapped under rubble of the skyscraper flung towards the ground during her rematch with the Falling Devil.
