## [series=gachiakuta | page=Information_Broker_Arc | section=Manga] Manga

The Information Broker Arc is the eighth story arc of the Gachiakuta manga series. After learning that the Choker Maker likely possesses a piece of the ancient Watchman Series, a chase unfolds for the Information Broker between the Cleaners and the Raiders. Said to have the greatest wealth of knowledge on the Ground, Kuro holds many secrets within him, and something lies in wait for both groups at the end of the highway.

## [series=gachiakuta | page=First_Job_Arc | section=Manga] Manga

The First Job Arc is the ninth story arc of the Gachiakuta manga series. After a brief but relevant chat with Corvus, Rudo now identifying as "Rudo Surebrec the Cleaner", is sent on a new mission by Semiu, his very first mission as a Cleaner alone, but he is assisted by Tamsy and some Supporters. Unfortunately, the Cleaners' job takes a turn for the worse as Follo's emotions get the better of him.

## [series=gachiakuta | page=Doll_Festival_Arc | section=Manga] Manga

The Doll Festival Arc is the tenth story arc of the Gachiakuta manga series. With the new information Kuro gave them about the Choker Maker's location, the Cleaners decide to go on a new mission to Andio on October 31st, where the vibrant Doll Festival is taking place. Their job is to search for the white crow, which the Information Broker claimed would lead them to the Choker Maker. Unfortunately, the fun goes south when an unexpected individual declares himself the Supreme Ruler of the Ground.
