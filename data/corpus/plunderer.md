## [series=plunderer | arc=special-service | ch=30-53] The Fake Schmelman revealed

The "Schmelman Bach" running the Special Service is a clone, not the original.
The real villain is Firenda, a 300-year-old scientist who has been disguised as
a soldier named Nikola the entire time. Firenda created the Schmelman clone as a
puppet to ruin the real Schmelman's legacy. Her ability is to steal and copy
anyone's appearance and abilities on touch. Her Count represents the number of
times she has stolen from others, and it is enormous. She has kept the real
Schmelman locked in a pocket dimension called the Ende, torturing him
psychologically for 300 years.

## [series=plunderer | arc=special-service | ch=30-53] Gespenst — Pele's true identity

Pele Poporo, the short comic-relief soldier, is actually Gespenst Zerlegen, the
7th Legendary Ace, known as the Piercing Baron. He is a Colonel General of the
Special Service. His ability is to read minds, and he has been spying on everyone
the whole time. Underneath the performance he genuinely cares about Lynn.

## [series=plunderer | arc=special-service | ch=30-53] Tokikaze climbs out of the Abyss

Sakai Tokikaze, Licht's adoptive brother and Hina's father, has survived 300
years in the Abyss. He collected four of the seven Ballots, giving him a
majority. He uses the Althing votes to cut Althea's food supply, annul the
nuclear ban, and declare war between the Abyss and Althea. His goal is to force
Althea to feel the suffering of the Abyss. He kidnaps Hina. His blade can now cut
through dimensions.

## [series=plunderer | arc=special-service | ch=30-53] Licht and Tokikaze meet in the rain

Licht and Tokikaze reunite in heavy rain. Licht cries and apologizes for nearly
killing Tokikaze 300 years ago. Tokikaze hugs him, then draws his sword and walks
away. Their goals are incompatible. War is coming.

## [series=plunderer | arc=last-war-part-1 | ch=54-70] Firenda kills the Clone Schmelman

Firenda kills the clone Schmelman efficiently and without ceremony. As he dies he
calls her "Mama." Hina slaps Firenda across the face. Firenda then drops the
Nikola disguise and reveals she has plundered the abilities of all seven
Legendary Aces, including Schmelman's ice, Jail's iron, Tokikaze's speed, and
Alan's fire. She is the most powerful person alive.

## [series=plunderer | arc=last-war-part-1 | ch=54-70] Tokikaze forced berserk

Firenda injects Tokikaze with a drug that forces his bloodlust gene active. He
goes berserk and becomes a weapon aimed at Licht. Eins, a Class A clone who loves
Tokikaze, fights the drug's control.

## [series=plunderer | arc=last-war-part-1 | ch=54-70] The real Schmelman in the Ende

The group finds the real Schmelman in the Ende. He looks like Japan 300 years
ago, preserved and frozen in time. He sits in a classroom reading a picture book.
The Class A students sit in their old seats and ask him to read to them. He cries,
and he does.

## [series=plunderer | arc=last-war-part-1 | ch=54-70] Licht masters the violence

Firenda makes her last stand. She freezes Licht solid and beats Jail with his own
ability. Licht breaks free: his hair turns black as he fuses Schmelman's
bloodlust genes into his own Count instead of fighting them, mastering the
violence rather than being ruled by it.

## [series=plunderer | arc=last-war-part-1 | ch=54-70] Mizuka shoots Firenda

Mizuka shoots Firenda and says that a heartless person like her can never defeat
Licht, because he has something she can never steal. That thing is love,
connection, and the people who believe in him — it cannot be plundered. Tokikaze
delivers the killing blow and Firenda falls. She spent 300 years stealing and
died with nothing.

## [series=plunderer | arc=last-war-part-2 | ch=71-82] Schmelman ends the Althing

The only real solution is to destroy the Althing entirely: no Althing means no
Counts, no Abyss, and no hierarchy. Someone must remain inside the Ende to sustain
the dismantling. Schmelman volunteers, saying he designed this world and he will
end it. Class A protests. He says goodbye and Althea begins to descend.

## [series=plunderer | arc=last-war-part-2 | ch=71-82] The sky island comes down

The sky island Althea comes down slowly and gently, settling rather than
crashing. Abyss children see direct sunlight for the first time. Two worlds that
were separated for 300 years begin to reunite.

## [series=plunderer | arc=epilogue | ch=82-82] Three years later

Chapter 82 is titled "A World of Peace, Happiness, and Boring" and takes place
three years later, in New Calendar Year 3. Former Special Service soldiers
complain the world is too peaceful; one admits she wants to commit murder just to
feel something, though the subtext is that they are quietly relieved. Gespenst
tells Lynn that if she wants a family they should start properly, and Lynn is
visibly happy. Taketora is at home failing to change his newborn son's diaper.
Class A clones work alongside their originals rebuilding the former Abyss.

## [series=plunderer | arc=epilogue | ch=82-82] Licht and Jail's rematch

Licht and Jail have their promised rematch, with no winner shown. Before fighting,
Jail asks why Licht only fights him at full strength. Licht answers that Jail is
his best rival and there is no one else he would insult by holding back. Jail is
moved but will not say so.

## [series=plunderer | arc=epilogue | ch=82-82] The final scene

Hina gives birth inside the Ende. Licht and Tokikaze argue outside the surgery
room about who holds the baby first and almost draw swords. Hina walks out and
shuts them both down, then calls in the man she asked to wait inside. It is
Schmelman: he survived and is alive in the Ende. He holds his great-grandchild
and cries. The baby has his eyes and his hair color. He says he hopes the peace
keeps going forever. The child is born without a Count — no number, just a life.
