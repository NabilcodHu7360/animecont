## MOVEMENT ONE — THE BAIT

[VISUAL: a paper-screen room at night, wisteria in bloom outside]

It begins not with a sword but with a sacrifice. Blind and dying, Kagaya Ubuyashiki — leader of the Demon Slayers — welcomes Muzan into his home, and calmly tells him the one thing the Demon King cannot bear to hear: that he will never be eternal, because the only thing that lasts is human feeling. [ch.137] [VISUAL: a house consumed by fire from within, screens glowing orange]

Then he detonates his own mansion, killing himself and his family to wound Muzan. [ch.138] In the smoke, Tamayo binds Muzan in flesh and injects him with a drug centuries in the making, and Gyomei's flail takes the Demon King's head. [ch.138]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: countless rooms and staircases folded at impossible angles]

Kagaya's true plan is revealed: he used precognition to lure Muzan into a battlefield the Slayers control, and made a boy — Kiriya, age eight — the new leader before he died. [ch.139][ch.144] The countdown starts. They only need to hold Muzan until dawn. [ch.147]

## MOVEMENT FOUR — THE DESTROYER AND THE PLANT

[MUSIC CUE: heavy percussion, then a strange, mournful stillness]

[HOST] Tanjiro and Giyu face Akaza — the demon who killed Rengoku — and this time Tanjiro is no longer weak. [ch.146][ch.147] [VISUAL: two sets of footprints circling each other on a stone floor]

Akaza senses "fighting spirit" to predict every attack, an unbeatable read. [ch.150] [VISUAL: deep snow, a bear's tracks, a single set of human footprints beside them]

So Tanjiro remembers his father felling a bear without a flicker of fear — and enters the Transparent World, a selfless state with no fighting spirit at all. [ch.151][ch.152] A boy who reads like a *plant*. And he beheads Upper Rank Three. [ch.152]

[VISUAL: a blade moving through a body that cannot sense it coming]

But Akaza won't die — his headless body keeps fighting, trying to relocate his own weak point like Muzan. [ch.153] Then a voice from his past reaches him: his human name, Hakuji. [ch.153] [VISUAL: a poor Edo tenement alley in rain, one paper lantern lit]

The manga stops the fight to tell his whole life — a boy who stole medicine for his dying father, found a family in a dojo master and his daughter Koyuki, and lost them all to poison. [ch.154][ch.155] [VISUAL: a dojo floor scattered with broken practice swords]

He massacred the killers and became a demon to forget. [ch.155] [VISUAL: cherry petals falling on a dojo porch]

Now, remembering, Akaza destroys his *own* regenerating body, chooses to lose, and walks into Hell with Koyuki's spirit welcoming him home. [ch.156] He dies human. [ch.157]
