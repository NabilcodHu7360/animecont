## ACT ONE — THE RACE BEGINS

Steven Steel announces the Steel Ball Run: a nine-stage horse race across America for fifty million dollars. [The Steel Ball Run Race] [VISUAL: two steel spheres resting in an open palm, catching desert light]

Among the field is **Gyro Zeppeli**, whose spinning Steel Balls do the impossible — when the paraplegic ex-jockey **Johnny Joestar** touches one, he stands, for a moment. [The Steel Ball Run Race] Desperate for that miracle, Johnny enters the race, and Gyro gives him his first hint about the Spin. [The Steel Ball Run Race]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: a canyon trail at speed, dust hanging in the air behind]

The race is a war of technique, luck, and endurance — Gyro seizing first by turning his cape into a sail. [The Race Begins] [VISUAL: a reliquary box open in the dirt, straw packing disturbed]

But it's more than a race: contestants are murdering each other, because hidden along the route are the **Corpse Parts** — the scattered body of a saint, each piece granting power. [The West: First Battles] [VISUAL: an arm wrapped in bandages, faint light bleeding through]

Johnny discovers his own left arm now holds one. [The West: First Battles]

## ACT TWO — STANDS AND THE SAINT'S CORPSE

[MUSIC CUE: tense, percussive — the first Stand battles]

[HOST] [VISUAL: a strange handprint burned into desert rock, heat shimmer above it]

The desert stage reveals the truth. [VISUAL: nail-shaped projectiles embedded in a cactus, humming]

Attacked by assassins hired to kill Gyro, Johnny awakens his own Stand — **Tusk** — inside a Devil's Palm, the phenomenon that grants Stands. [The West: First Battles]

And behind it all stands the President of the United States: **Funny Valentine**, who sponsored the entire race to secretly collect every Corpse Part using Joseph of Arimathea's map. [The West: First Battles]

[VISUAL: a man in a star-spangled coat studying an ancient map]

[VISUAL: a lone water tower on an endless plain, a single rider approaching]

The middle of the race becomes a gauntlet of Valentine's agents and rival racers — the time-rewinding gunslinger **Ringo Roadagain**, whom Gyro beats only after finally finding his own drive to win [The Midwest: Struggle for the Saint's Corpse]; and **Sandman**, whose betrayal pushes Johnny to upgrade Tusk to ACT2 by grasping the Golden Rectangle — the infinite-rotation proportion at the Spin's heart. [The Midwest] [VISUAL: a presidential railcar interior, velvet curtains drawn, no one in frame]

Meanwhile Lucy Steel, Steven's wife, infiltrates Valentine's world to steal the Corpse from within. [The Midwest]

## ACT THREE — D4C

[MUSIC CUE: everything tightens — the President enters]

[HOST] Valentine's own Stand finally shows itself: **Dirty Deeds Done Dirt Cheap** — D4C — which lets him step between parallel universes, pulling in alternate versions of people and objects. [The North-East: The Hunt Concludes] It's why no one can tell who's attacking; there are many Valentines, many Diegos. [The North-East]

[VISUAL: a corridor that repeats itself impossibly into the distance]

Lucy, now the host of the Corpse's Head, is cornered by Valentine. [The North-East] And Johnny, in a garbage-dump trial of his own guilt over his brother Nicholas's death, learns to shoot *himself* — unlocking a wormhole ability that turns Tusk into a weapon against the President. [The North-East]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: a steam train crossing a trestle bridge at dusk, sparks trailing]

The endgame boards a train to the map's final "X." [The West Coast: The Power the Saint's Corpse] [VISUAL: a train corridor, two doors open, no one there]

Diego and Hot Pants both die trying to kill Valentine, whose new power — **Love Train** — redirects all "harm" in the world away from himself, making him invincible. [The West Coast]

## ACT FOUR — THE SHORTEST ROUTE WAS A DETOUR

[MUSIC CUE: strings swell, tragic and huge]

[VISUAL: a perfect golden spiral drawn in light against black]

[HOST] Gyro finds the answer in the **Golden Spin** — the Spin's highest form. [The West Coast] [VISUAL: a steel sphere spinning so fast the air distorts around it]

His new Stand, **Ball Breaker**, drives senescence into Valentine's cells, bypassing Love Train entirely. [The West Coast]

But a freak coincidence makes Gyro's rotation imperfect, and Valentine survives. [The West Coast] Dying, Gyro passes Johnny his final lesson: *"The shortest route was a detour. It was a detour that was our shortest path."* [The West Coast]

[VISUAL: a man falling from his horse, a steel ball rolling away in the dust]

[VISUAL: an empty saddle, stirrups swinging, horizon tilted]

Johnny understands. Kicking off his horse in a Golden Scale movement, he unlocks **Tusk ACT4** — a rotation that follows Valentine across every dimension he flees to. [The West Coast] The President, cornered at last, tries to bargain by offering to bring Gyro back. [The West Coast] [VISUAL: a spent cartridge landing on a wooden floor]

Johnny wins the final shootout, bids his friend's ghost farewell, and kills Funny Valentine. [The West Coast]

## ACT FIVE — A LAST ENEMY

[MUSIC CUE: one more threat, low and cold]

[VISUAL: a bank vault door standing open, nothing inside]

[HOST] But the Corpse is stolen by one last foe: an **alternate Diego Brando**, whom Valentine recruited from another world, wielding the time-stopping **THE WORLD**. [New York City: A Last Enemy] Their duel ends on the Brooklyn Bridge — Diego wins the race and reaches the safe. [New York City]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: the Brooklyn Bridge at dawn, empty of traffic]

It's Lucy who ends it, killing the alternate Diego by fusing his head with the original's. [Conclusion] Johnny survives, and in the quiet after, boards a boat to carry Gyro's body home to his family — meeting his future wife, Rina Higashikata, along the way. [Conclusion] A last prayer, a smile, and he sails for Europe to bury his friend. [Conclusion]
