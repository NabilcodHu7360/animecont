## ACT ONE — THE DEVIL ON HIS SHOULDER

The night he wakes, Asta bolts for the door to save Yami — and freezes mid-stride, pinned by a stranger's shadow. [Return to the Clover Kingdom] The man is Nacht Faust, secret vice-captain of the Black Bulls, a devil perched on his shoulder like a second conscience. His verdict is blunt: a human who can't beat *him* will never rescue Yami. [Return to the Clover Kingdom]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: a stone chamber ringed with bones, one shaft of light falling from far above]

So Nacht drags Asta into forbidden ground. In a chamber ringed with the bones of everyone who failed before him, Asta faces the devil inside his own grimoire — and refuses to kill it. [Return to the Clover Kingdom] [VISUAL: a small horned silhouette curled in the dark, not attacking]

Because Asta feels something Nacht insists doesn't exist: a devil who isn't trying to kill him. The devil remembers a woman named Richita — the only person who never flinched at the sight of him, who gave him a name. [Return to the Clover Kingdom] *Liebe.* [Return to the Clover Kingdom] She died sealing him inside a five-leaf grimoire to keep him from Lucifero. Asta doesn't conquer Liebe. He asks him to be his friend — a contract between equals. [Return to the Clover Kingdom]

[MUSIC CUE: the drum joins a rising string line]

[HOST] They win every fight. And it isn't enough.

## ACT FOUR — THE FINAL WIZARD KING

[MUSIC CUE: a slow, cold, church-like organ]

[HOST] [VISUAL: a celebration hall frozen mid-moment, confetti hanging motionless in the air]

A year of peace, and then it shatters at Asta's own awards ceremony. [Synopsis]

Lucius wears Julius's face but isn't him. He freezes time, declares himself humanity's savior, and lays out his vision: destroy every human, remake them identical, and rule as the final Wizard King over a peace with no differences left to fight over. [Synopsis] To him, a magicless boy is a flaw in creation that has to be erased.

[VISUAL: a gentle nun's face, light pouring into her eyes as something behind them goes out]

Then he does the cruelest thing in the series. He turns Sister Lily — the woman Asta has loved and proposed to since childhood — into an angelic Paladin, and orders her to tell Asta to die for world peace. [Synopsis] Lucius slashes him across the chest while he stands frozen in horror, and Lily erases him with Spatial Magic. [Synopsis] Judgment Day, Lucius announces, comes in seven days.

[MUSIC CUE: silence, then a single wave breaking]

But Asta isn't dead. He washes up on a foreign shore.

[VISUAL: black volcanic sand at dawn, waves pulling back from a shoreline] [Synopsis]

## ACT FIVE — THE LAND OF THE SUN

[HOST] He wakes in Yami's homeland — the Land of the Sun — found by its magicless Shogun, Ryuya, who can *see* that Asta can't win against Lucius yet. [The Land of the Sun] [VISUAL: a training ground at first light, wooden posts standing in mist]

So Asta trains. [VISUAL: a single grain of rice split cleanly in two on a wooden board]

He learns Zetten: magic concentrated to a razor's point and released — and in his hands, the ultimate counter. [The Land of the Sun]

[VISUAL: a bamboo stalk sliced so cleanly it hasn't yet fallen]

His sparring partner despises him — Ichika, Yami's own sister, who believes Yami slaughtered their clan. [The Land of the Sun] [VISUAL: a burned-out compound under falling snow, nothing left standing]

The truth is worse and kinder: their abusive father force-fed *Ichika* the clan's bloodlust drug, and *she* was the one who killed them all. Yami took the blame and vanished to spare her the guilt. [The Land of the Sun]

When Lily and the Paladins invade the Land of the Sun, Asta returns transformed. [The Land of the Sun] He cuts a Paladin's magic away and reverts him to human. [The Land of the Sun] [VISUAL: a rosary broken, beads scattering across stone]

He reaches Lily — who breaks down and admits she never wanted to hurt him, that Lucius overwrites what a person truly believes — and frees her with Anti-Magic. [The Land of the Sun] Her confession reveals the endgame: on Judgment Day, Lucius seizes all the magic in the world. [The Land of the Sun]
