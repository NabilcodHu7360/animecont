## ACT ONE — THE GAME

[VISUAL: a map of Japan divided into ten walled zones, each glowing faintly]

The man in Geto's body has a name — **Kenjaku** — and a plan: force the evolution of humanity by merging everyone in Japan with Master Tengen. [Perfect Preparation] The ritual that begins it is the **Culling Game**: a battle royale of sorcerers, ancient and modern, sealed in ten colonies across Japan. [Perfect Preparation]

[VISUAL: a hospital bed, folded blanket, morning light through blinds]

For Megumi, it's personal — his sister Tsumiki is trapped inside, so he pulls Yuji back into the fight. [Itadori's Extermination] [VISUAL: a sealed black cube on an altar, faint light at its seams]

But there's a bigger prize: Master Tengen reveals a "back gate" to the Prison Realm that can free **Satoru Gojo** — openable only by the cursed technique of a player called the Angel, Hana Kurusu. [Perfect Preparation]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: a traditional clan estate burning from within, gates thrown open]

Before any of that, tragedy strikes the Zenin clan. Ordered dead by their own father, Maki and Mai make their last stand — and Mai, dying, gives Maki all her cursed energy and one request: *"Destroy everything."* [Perfect Preparation] [VISUAL: a katana driven into a tatami floor, the room silent]

Maki keeps the promise, annihilating the entire clan. [Perfect Preparation] Elsewhere, Master Yaga is executed, leaving Gakuganji the secret of the cursed corpse as "a curse from me to you." [Perfect Preparation]

## ACT TWO — THE COLONIES

[MUSIC CUE: tense, percussive — colony combat theme]

[HOST] The players scatter across colonies, each fight a duel of strange techniques.

[VISUAL: a courtroom gallery, empty benches, a gavel resting on wood]

Yuji recruits the lawyer **Higuruma**, whose courtroom Domain puts him on trial — but Higuruma, unable to prosecute an innocent man, throws the fight and adds the rule that saves them all. [Tokyo No. 1 Colony: Angry Man] [VISUAL: thousands of receipts swirling through an empty shopping arcade]

Megumi traps the receipt-hoarder **Reggie** in his Chimera Shadow Garden and wins. [Tokyo No. 1 Colony] And in the wings lurks **Takaba**, whose Comedian technique turns anything he finds funny into reality — strong enough to rival Gojo, and he has no idea he has it. [Tokyo No. 1 Colony]

[VISUAL: a courtroom materializing out of nothing around two fighters]

[VISUAL: a ruined city crossroads, cursed haze pooling in the streets]

In Sendai, **Yuta** cuts through the strongest players, giving the four-hundred-year-hungry Ryu the "dessert" of a real fight. [Sendai Colony: The Height of the Feast] [VISUAL: a pachinko parlour blazing with light, every machine running at once]

Hakari beats the manga-artist Charles and the electric ancient **Kashimo** with his pachinko Domain and infinite cursed energy. [Tokyo No. 2 Colony: Incredible Luck] [VISUAL: a broken cursed tool lying on volcanic rock]

And Maki — now Toji's equal — ends a reincarnated, vengeful **Naoya** for good. [Sakurajima Colony: Resurrection]

## ACT THREE — WHAT KENJAKU REALLY WANTS

[MUSIC CUE: strings swell, ominous]

[VISUAL: a war room map covered in pins, a hand hovering over it]

[HOST] While the players bleed, Kenjaku moves. Months earlier, he revealed sorcery to national governments and convinced a foreign military to invade the Game — then released curses to massacre the soldiers, filling the colonies with the cursed energy his merger needs. [Offering to the Unknown]

[VISUAL: an ancient tomb complex beneath a school, torch-lit and vast]

Above the Tombs of the Star, Yuki finally corners Tengen with the questions she's carried for years — until Kenjaku invades. [Blood, Stars, and Oil] [VISUAL: a blood-red sky over an ancient burial mound]

Choso intercepts his own father; Kenjaku shows him the vision of turning all Japan into a single new entity, and admits he does it because it *sounds fun*. [Blood, Stars, and Oil] Yuki and Choso fight him with everything — and still, Tengen is seized. [Blood, Stars, and Oil] Yuki, bisected, compresses her own body into a black hole

[VISUAL: a point of absolute black tearing light inward around it] rather than die quietly. [Blood, Stars, and Oil]

## ACT FOUR — THE SOUL IN THE DARK

[MUSIC CUE: everything drops to near silence, a single dissonant tone]

[HOST] This is where Jujutsu Kaisen breaks your heart.

[VISUAL: a hospital room, an empty bed, the window open]

"Tsumiki" reveals herself as an imposter — the thousand-year-old sorcerer **Yorozu** — and Sukuna makes his move. [Fearsome Womb: Recurrence] [VISUAL: two hands clasped, a thin line of light binding the wrists]

Tricking Yuji with a binding vow, he tears off a finger and forces it into Megumi, incarnating his soul into Megumi's body. [Fearsome Womb] Yuji can only watch as his best friend is taken. [Fearsome Womb]

[VISUAL: a boy reaching out as another's eyes go cold and marked]

[VISUAL: black water, still, something submerged just beneath the surface]

Sukuna submerges Megumi's soul in evil in the Bath, then goes to kill Yorozu — and Tsumiki with her. [Fearsome Womb] Yorozu, obsessed with him for a thousand years, wants to *marry* him; Sukuna only wants to stain Megumi's soul with his sister's blood. [Fearsome Womb] She brings out her greatest creation; he crushes it with Mahoraga's adaptation. [Fearsome Womb] With Tsumiki's end, Megumi's soul drowns within Sukuna. [Fearsome Womb]

## ACT FIVE — THE STRONGEST RETURNS

[MUSIC CUE: the piano from the open returns — resolving, enormous]

[HOST] The sorcerers free Gojo at last. [Satoru Gojo's Comeback] Hana survived Sukuna thanks to Rin and Takaba, and her role ends the moment Gojo is unsealed. [Satoru Gojo's Comeback] Unsealed, Gojo confronts Kenjaku and Sukuna — and all three agree to settle it on December 24th. [Satoru Gojo's Comeback]

[VISUAL: a beam of light carving a corridor straight through a city skyline]

On Christmas Eve, Gojo fires a 200% Hollow Purple from Shibuya to Shinjuku, taking both Sukuna's arms. [The Battle of the Strongest] [VISUAL: two domes of opposing light expanding against each other over a city]

What follows is the fight the whole series built toward — domain against domain, Unlimited Void against Malevolent Shrine, each man healing his own burnt-out technique in ways that shouldn't be possible. [The Battle of the Strongest]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: a spinning wheel of iron hanging in the air above a broken street]

Gojo gains the upper hand — until **Mahoraga**, adapting through Megumi's soul, destroys the Infinity itself. [The Battle of the Strongest] Gojo unleashes Unlimited Hollow and heavily wounds Sukuna — and it looks won. [The Battle of the Strongest] [VISUAL: a clean line cut through empty air, reality parting along it]

But Sukuna copies Mahoraga's "slash the world" adaptation to bypass the Infinity, and with a single binding-vow cut, **kills Satoru Gojo.** [The Battle of the Strongest]

## ACT SIX — THE RAID

[MUSIC CUE: war drums, relentless]

[VISUAL: snow falling on a ruined intersection, one figure standing at its centre]

[HOST] With the strongest dead, the sorcerers raid a weakened Sukuna. [The Battle of the Strongest] Kashimo forces him into his true form and is overpowered. [The Battle of the Strongest] Then Yuji and Higuruma arrive. [The Battle of the Strongest]

Elsewhere, Kenjaku hunts the last players to end the Game — until **Takaba** is sent against him. [Foolish Survivor] [VISUAL: a spotlit stage in an empty theatre, one microphone standing]

The comedian's technique proves unbeatable once he rededicates himself to making people laugh, and the two perform a joyous rendition of Momotarō. [Foolish Survivor] It's a distraction — and while Kenjaku laughs, **Yuta** decapitates him. [Foolish Survivor] Even headless, Kenjaku promises his legacy will endure. [Foolish Survivor]

[HOST] This is where the anime hasn't taken you. Gojo is dead. Kenjaku is beheaded. And the final arc — the Shinjuku Showdown — is nothing but the last, climactic clash between the jujutsu sorcerers and the King of Curses. [Shinjuku Showdown Arc: Manga]
