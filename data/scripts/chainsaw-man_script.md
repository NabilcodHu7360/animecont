## MOVEMENT ONE — THE END OF PART 1

### The Assassins

After Reze, Denji's fights hit the news, and Makima delivers the bad news with a smile: assassins are coming to Japan to take his heart. [International Assassins arc] [VISUAL: a departures board flickering with cities that have nothing in common]

They come from everywhere — three "immortal" American brothers with a Skin Devil that steals the faces of the dead, a Soviet hunter and his student Tolka with a Curse Devil's nail, the German doll-master Santa Claus, and Quanxi — a woman Kishibe flatly calls the strongest human alive — with her harem of Fiends. [International Assassins arc]

[MUSIC CUE: nervous, ticking percussion]

[VISUAL: a department store escalator stopped mid-flight, mannequins scattered]

It collapses into a slaughter inside a department store, and then somewhere worse: Santa Claus drags everyone into Hell itself, where a Primal Fear — the Darkness Devil — severs their hands and cuts through them like they're nothing, killing Quanxi, Beam, and nearly Denji. [International Assassins arc] [VISUAL: a doorway of absolute darkness, hands reaching from the other side]

Makima teleports in, uses Tolka's corpse to force everyone back to the real world, and revives Denji with her blood — asking him, again, if he'll save her. He answers: "Woof." [International Assassins arc]

[BEAT]

The fight ends with Quanxi's Cosmo trapping Santa Claus in an endless "Halloween," and a whole crowd left able to say only that one word. [International Assassins arc]

### The Worst Possible Death

[MUSIC CUE: everything softens — a quiet, domestic warmth that you already know is doomed]

Then the manga does something cruel: it gives you peace.

[VISUAL: a family grave on a windswept hill, three sets of footprints in the grass] Denji, Power, and Aki travel to Aki's family grave, and for a few pages they're just a family. [Gun Devil arc] But the Future Devil has already told Aki the ending — that Denji will kill him and Power, and something terrible will follow. [Gun Devil arc]

[VISUAL: a long office corridor, one door open at the far end]

Desperate to protect them, Aki begs Makima for a contract to grow stronger. Instead she reveals what she is. [Gun Devil arc] [VISUAL: an enormous gun barrel emerging from cloud cover over a city]

When the American president sacrifices a year of his people's lifespans to summon the Gun Devil to kill her, Makima — revealed as the **Control Devil** — takes the head-shot without flinching and tortures the Gun Devil to death with borrowed power. [Gun Devil arc] [VISUAL: an apartment door left ajar, snow blowing into the hallway]

Then she turns Aki into the Gun Fiend and sends him home. Denji, begged by dying civilians to save them, kills the thing wearing his best friend's face. [Gun Devil arc] The Future Devil's verdict: Aki died in the worst possible way for Denji.

### The Dog and the Devil

[MUSIC CUE: low, cold, inevitable]

[VISUAL: an empty apartment, two unwashed plates, morning light]

[HOST] Grief-hollowed, Denji goes to Makima and asks to be her dog. [Control Devil arc] [VISUAL: a spray of blood frozen against a white wall]

So she shoots Power dead in front of him — "Bang" — to break his bond with Pochita, and reveals he murdered his own father. [Control Devil arc] Then she takes control of him.

[VISUAL: a hallway of kneeling figures, all waiting for one boy to wake up]

[VISUAL: a circle of candles burning low on a concrete floor]

But Kishibe's Anti-Makima squad sacrifices themselves to cast her into Hell, and Pochita — the true Chainsaw Devil, the Hero of Hell whose chainsaws can erase Devils from existence — finally takes the wheel. [Control Devil arc] Makima survives on a contract with the Prime Minister that shunts her wounds onto every Japanese citizen. She wants to use Chainsaw Man to erase death, war, and hunger from the world. [Control Devil arc]

[VISUAL: a city street torn open, chainsaw scars carved through the asphalt]

The battle is a citywide massacre of Hybrids. And then the twist: the "Denji" Makima has been fighting was a decoy Pochita, torn from his own heart — because Makima recognizes people by scent, not by face. The real Denji rises from the corpses and cuts her with a Blood Chainsaw. [Control Devil arc] [VISUAL: a small kitchen at night, a single plate set at a table for two]

He takes her home. He cooks her. He eats her — an act he calls, in his own logic, love. [Control Devil arc]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: a child's hand holding an adult's, seen from behind, on a crowded street]

Later, a small child bites his finger. Kishibe reveals she's the reincarnated Control Devil, memories gone, brought from China — a devil who, Pochita says, always wanted a family and went about it the worst way imaginable. Denji names her, or rather she names herself: **Nayuta.** [Control Devil arc]

[MUSIC CUE: a fragile, hopeful piano — the first soft thing that feels earned]

## MOVEMENT TWO — PART 2: THE WAR DEVIL

### A New Kid, A New Devil

[HOST] Part 2 hands the story to someone new. Asa Mitaka — friendless, unlucky — accidentally crushes her class's pet devil-chicken, and it ruins her life. [Justice Devil arc] When the class president, contracted to the Justice Devil, tries to kill her for it, a bird revives Asa — as the host of the **War Devil, Yoru.** [Justice Devil arc] [VISUAL: a school corridor, one locker door hanging open]

Yoru leaves half of Asa's brain intact to hide among humans, and declares her goal: make Chainsaw Man vomit up the Nuclear Weapons Devil. [Justice Devil arc]

[VISUAL: an empty classroom at dusk, one desk apart from the rows]

Asa just wants a friend. [VISUAL: two lunch trays on a rooftop, one untouched]

She finds one in Yuko — who loves her so much she contracts the Justice Devil and starts killing to "protect" her, until she's twisted into something monstrous and dies for it. [Justice Devil arc] Asa's first lesson in this world: kindness here gets people killed.

### Falling, and a Church

[MUSIC CUE: melancholy, off-kilter]

[VISUAL: a wall of glowing billboards, one figure standing beneath them alone]

Denji, meanwhile, is a lonely celebrity. He dates Asa without either of them knowing who the other is, [VISUAL: an aquarium tunnel at night, blue light rippling over empty benches]

and they beat the Eternity Devil in an aquarium together — a genuinely sweet disaster of a first date. [Dating Denji arc] But Nayuta, wary of the War Devil, erases their memories to keep them apart. [Dating Denji arc]

Then the Primal Fears escalate. [VISUAL: an apartment block seen from directly below, windows receding into sky]

The Falling Devil turns a whole apartment block's residents into a single falling horror, drags Asa and Denji toward Hell, and forces Asa through her worst memories. [Falling Devil arc] Denji saves her mid-fall — and when she tells him no woman would ever love a man with a chainsaw for a head, it wounds him badly enough that he starts falling too. [Falling Devil arc] [VISUAL: a figure at the far end of an alley, out of focus]

A mysterious "second Chainsaw Man" keeps appearing at the edges. [Falling Devil arc]

[VISUAL: an empty banquet table set for hundreds, every plate untouched]

[HOST] And behind it all: Fami, the Famine Devil, chasing the Prophecy of Nostradamus — and a **Chainsaw Man Church**, publicly fronted by a fan but truly run by Fami, weaponizing the public's worship. [Chainsaw Man Church arc] [VISUAL: a burning house, a dog's leash on the fence outside]

When Denji finally refuses them and the Weapons burn down his home and his pets, he pulls his cord and becomes Chainsaw Man again — and the public that once cheered him turns on him, stabbing and burning their own hero. [Chainsaw Man Church arc]

### The Detention Center

[MUSIC CUE: grinding, mechanical]

[VISUAL: rows of identical steel boxes in a cold warehouse, one open]

Denji is sedated and shipped to a Devil Detention Center to be dismembered — chopped into boxes. [Aging Devil arc] Asa, Yoru, and Fami break in to rescue him, and Haruka, digging through the boxes for his idol, finds the head — and learns his hero is just Denji. [Aging Devil arc] They escape and reassemble him.

[VISUAL: a lacquered plate under a serving cloche, the cloche not yet lifted]

Then Barem serves Denji sushi. On the plate: Nayuta's severed head. [Aging Devil arc]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: a chainsaw cord hanging still, gripped in a shaking fist]

Denji breaks. Pochita takes over completely and goes on a rampage, and Public Safety fights back with the Aging Devil — until, trapped in a realm where no one can age or die, Asa's own regret over her mother reaches Denji. [Aging Devil arc] He remembers his dead — Aki, Power, Reze, Nayuta — and chooses to live, feeding Pochita until he can escape and trap the Aging Devil in its own world. [Aging Devil arc]

### The War Devil's Endgame

[MUSIC CUE: full, apocalyptic, no mercy]

[HOST] [VISUAL: an antique globe, half the continents scorched away]

The truth surfaces: "Fami" was often the **Death Devil** in disguise, and Yoru's real plan is monstrous — erase Death, then start a never-ending World War II where undying humans suffer forever. [War Devil arc] To stop it, Denji reluctantly sides with Death herself. [War Devil arc]

[VISUAL: a spear made of a flag, falling from the moon toward earth]

Yoru descends from Hell to fight Pochita for the fate of mankind. [War Devil arc] She sacrifices thousands of lives, turns the moon's American flag into a homing spear, lands a nuclear punch that knocks Pochita out. [War Devil arc] And Pochita, consuming the Death Devil, erases the concept of mortality itself. [War Devil arc]

[VISUAL: two boys in an alley, one chasing something he's been chasing his whole life]

In the end, it comes down to what always mattered: Asa, deep in her own trauma, finally reaches her dead mother and makes peace. [War Devil arc] Yoru, at the last second, can't kill the boy — because Asa can't bear to see a bird die, and Yoru swerves rather than finish him. [War Devil arc]

[VISUAL: a small heart-shaped shape cupped in two hands, glowing faintly]

And then Pochita makes his choice. In a final embrace, he tears out his own heart and reveals his plan: to devour himself and create a world where he never existed. [War Devil arc] Denji begs him not to go. Pochita throws one last peace sign and erases himself. [War Devil arc]

[MUSIC CUE: strip everything back to that first feedback note, now gentle]

[VISUAL: a corrugated shack at dawn, one dog bowl by the door]

Denji wakes in his old shack, sick heart and all — betrayed by the Debt Collector, saved by Power, inducted into Public Safety by Nayuta, exactly as it once began. [War Devil arc] Sent to a devil near Fourth East High, he catches a falling Asa in his arms before she can trip on Bucky. She thanks him, runs off to play with her friends — and Denji, with one long look back, goes home. [War Devil arc]

[HOST] That's the story the anime hasn't touched: the death of everyone Denji loved, and a world remade so a devil could give him a softer start. It is, somehow, both the bleakest and the kindest ending Chainsaw Man could have.

[MUSIC CUE: out]
