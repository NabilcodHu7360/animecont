## MOVEMENT ONE — INFILTRATING HŌRAI

The surviving criminals and executioners ally to raid Hōrai for the Elixir of Life and escape — but Lord Tensen anticipates them, planning to offer them as sacrifices while their leader, Rien, prepares to sail to Japan and make the elixir from Japan's own people. [Hōrai Arc: Arc Chronology] [VISUAL: a map of an island drawn in the sand, split by a line]

The group splits into an Elixir Team and an Escape Team, and Gabimaru promises Mei he'll get her off the island to live a normal life. [Infiltrating Hōrai]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: six ornate palace gates standing open in a row, none of them safe]

The infiltration collapses immediately: the Tensen were warned by Chōbei, and each palace becomes an ambush for the Rite of Just Consumption. [Infiltrating Hōrai] [VISUAL: a corridor of red pillars vanishing into darkness]

There's no avoiding them now. The only way out is through.

## MOVEMENT TWO — THE SIX PALACES

[MUSIC CUE: percussive, urgent — six fights braided together]

[HOST] What follows is a gauntlet, each battle turning on the Tao-element matchups the survivors spent the whole series learning.

[VISUAL: a palace of interlocking stone reassembling itself mid-air]

Gabimaru and Yuzuriha face **Ran**, whose reconstructed palace and Tao Armor shrug off every hit — until Gabimaru's real plan lands: bait Ran into burning his own Tao, then have Yuzuriha strike the killing blow with her Earth Tao. [Battle in the Dō'in Palace] She spends the last Tao in her body to do it, knowing it may cost her life. [Battle in the Dō'in Palace] [VISUAL: a flowering vine growing from a wound in stone]

Gabimaru, meanwhile, pricks himself with a Kishikai stinger to steal Flower Tao's regeneration — sacrificing his humanity to survive. [Battle in the Dō'in Palace]

[VISUAL: two blades crossed on a lacquered floor, one snapped clean]

Elsewhere, Chōbei reveals his infiltration was a ruse — reuniting with his brother Tōma to turn on **Ju Fa and Tao Fa**. [Battle in the Bōchū Palace] [VISUAL: a discarded sword-guard on bloodied stone, light glancing off it]

Gantetsusai literally blinds himself, tearing out an eye to stop relying on sight and finally *sense* Tao. [Battle in the Bōchū Palace] [VISUAL: two silhouettes fusing into one wrong shape against a paper screen]

When the two Tensen merge into a single monster, the four men — Chōbei, Tōma, Gantetsusai, Fuchi — take it down together. [Battle in the Bōchū Palace]

[VISUAL: a courtyard of white sand raked into circles, one circle broken]

And in the Taisoku Palace, Shion — grieving his student Tenza — nearly gives his life to kill **Zhu Jin**, until Nurugai pulls him back, choosing survival over revenge. [Battle in the Taisoku Palace] [VISUAL: a blood trail leading away across white sand]

It's a mercy that will cost them: the still-living Zhu Jin crawls away toward the Banko. [Dire Straits]

## MOVEMENT THREE — ALL-OUT WAR

[MUSIC CUE: the braided fights collapse into one chaotic wall of drums]

[HOST] Just as the Tensen fall, Hōrai erupts into a three-way war. [All-Out War] A second landing party of Iwagakure shinobi, ordered by Shugen to kill the convicts, crashes in — while Rien's Dōshi army attacks everyone. [All-Out War]

[VISUAL: three armies tearing into each other under a collapsing golden sky]

[VISUAL: a standard planted in scorched earth, cloth torn by wind]

Shugen, fighting to keep the Yamada clan's balance, cuts down **Fuchi** — who dies having finally reached a kinder philosophy he wishes he could have shared. [All-Out War][Dire Straits] [VISUAL: an immense tree of writhing roots erupting through a palace roof]

Then Zhu Jin merges with the **Banko**, bursting out as a colossal plant-monster that will Arborify everyone by morning. [Dire Straits] [VISUAL: two broken formations of soldiers facing a single silhouette on a hill]

With the survivors scattered and dying, Jikka forces the two enemy landing parties to finally *cooperate* — pairing attackers and boosters to destroy the monster's five tandens. [Dire Straits]

## MOVEMENT FOUR — GABIMARU VS. GABIMARU

[MUSIC CUE: everything drops to two men breathing]

[HOST] At the Metal tanden, Gabimaru meets **Shija** — his own successor, who can predict his every move after years of studying him, and who wants nothing but to die fighting him at full strength. [Gabimaru vs. Gabimaru]

[VISUAL: a shattered mirror in a stone corridor, reflections misaligned]

Shija tries to break him with the cruelest weapon: the claim that his wife Yui was faking her love, that she doesn't really exist. [Gabimaru vs. Gabimaru] But when Shija instead *confirms* Yui is real, Gabimaru's stabilized emotions heal his failing body, and he finishes the fight. [Gabimaru vs. Gabimaru] Dying, Shija helps him anyway — signaling the other shinobi to kill each other, and asking, like a marriage vow, whether Gabimaru would fight them again if they were reborn. He says yes. [Gabimaru vs. Gabimaru]

[BEAT]

[VISUAL: dawn light spilling over an island, roots drinking it in]

Jikka fells the Banko, but sunlight revives it — so **Chōbei** makes the final call, using his vines to destroy all the tandens' roots at once, sacrificing himself. [Break Down, Come Apart] He crumbles away, finding peace with his parents in the afterlife, having at least been a good older brother to Tōma. [Break Down, Come Apart]

## MOVEMENT FIVE — DEPARTURE

[MUSIC CUE: the flute returns, mournful, over water]

[HOST] The island is dying, and only one ship remains. [Departure Arc: Arc Chronology] Gui Fa reveals the truth beneath everything: **Rien is Jofuku's wife**, using a puppet body, trying to turn all of Japan into Tan to revive her dead husband — and warns that only two men and one woman will survive. [Leaving the Island]

[VISUAL: a burning harbour at night, one ship silhouetted beyond the flames]

To clear the burning wreckage blocking the last ship, **Mei** transforms and sacrifices herself, sending an illusion to say goodbye to Gabimaru and Sagiri. [Leaving the Island]

[VISUAL: a girl's silhouette dissolving into light over black water]

[VISUAL: a ship's deck ablaze, masts falling, black water beyond]

On Rien's burning ship, the last survivors make their stand. [The Last Stand] Rien crushes Kiyomaru and Isuzu, and Gabimaru — grabbing her to burn her — hesitates at the image of Yui and begins to Arborify. [Hijacking the Ship][The Last Stand] It's **Sagiri** who saves him: beheading him, amputating his rooted limbs, and pressing her tanden to his to restore him. [The Last Stand]

[VISUAL: a gilded corpse laid in state, flowers long dead around it]

Then, understanding at last that Rien is a grieving *wife*, Gabimaru targets Jofuku's gilded corpse — but can't destroy it, sympathizing with a fellow married soul. [The Last Stand] Shugen finishes the strike, and Sagiri bisects Rien's ovule. [The Last Stand] Shugen dies from spending all his Tao. [The Last Stand] And when Rien — half-regenerated — hears Gabimaru say they both had someone they loved, she finally accepts Jofuku's death, heals them both, and reunites with her husband in the afterlife. [Arc Chronology (Departure)]
